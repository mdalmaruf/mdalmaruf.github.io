<section class="profile-intro">
  <p class="section-label">Research profile</p>
  <h2>From algorithms to systems that can be measured, trusted, and reused.</h2>
  <p>My research develops hardware-aware software methods, experimental testbeds, and hardware-in-the-loop techniques for embedded and edge systems. I earned my PhD and MASc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Ontario Tech University. My work on FeaMod received the IEEE IRI 2024 Best Paper Award.</p>
